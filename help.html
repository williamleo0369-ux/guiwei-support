使用帮助

记录与寻找

1. 点击新增，拍照或从相册选照片。
2. 选择区域与位置，例如“书房 → 桌面”。
3. 输入“电脑、鼠标、充电器、水杯”，也可使用系统键盘的听写。归位会拆分清单，确认后统一保存到所选位置。标签可留空。
4. 使用物品名、标签或位置搜索；点开物品可查看照片和位置，修改记录或移动位置。

归位当前不使用 AI 图片识别。系统听写由设备操作系统提供，不是归位的语音模型服务。

备份与换机

在“我的 → 数据与备份”导出完整 ZIP 备份，并确认文件已保存到你选择的安全位置。JSON 不包含照片，不能代替完整备份。卸载、清理设备或换机前务必保存完整备份。

在新设备选择完整备份文件并核对恢复提示。当前完整备份上限为 150 MB，还需足够的设备可用空间。文件损坏、照片缺失或版本不受支持时会拒绝恢复。不要把私人备份上传到 GitHub。

示例与草稿

“我的”提供示例清理，只删除未修改的示例物品。自己新增或修改过的记录、位置和草稿会保留。批量记录可保留草稿，下次打开继续填写。

登录与删除账号

Apple 和 Google 登录为可选功能。退出登录保留此设备上的收纳记录。删除账号请进入“我的”的账号管理，阅读提示并确认；可能需要重新验证登录。删除服务账号与删除设备上的收纳资料是分开的操作，按界面提示选择并提前备份。当前测试版在 Apple 账号删除后提供系统授权管理指引，自动撤销仍在接入验证。

反馈

通过本仓库 Issues 提交功能名称、应用版本、iOS 版本和不包含私人信息的复现步骤。不要填写密码、验证码、家庭地址、证件、未打码的账号信息或完整备份。公开问题对所有人可见。

Help

Record and find items

Take or select a photo, choose a room and storage location, then enter an item list such as “laptop, mouse, charger, cup”. System keyboard dictation also works. Review the split list and save all items to the selected location. Tags are optional. Search by item name, tag or location; open an item to view its photo or edit it.

Guiwei does not currently use AI image recognition. Keyboard dictation is provided by your operating system.

Backup and device changes

Export a full ZIP backup from Me → Data & Backup and confirm it has been saved safely. JSON exports do not include photos. Save a full backup before uninstalling, clearing your device or changing phones.

Select the full backup on the destination device and review the restore prompt. Backups currently have a 150 MB limit and require free device storage. Damaged, incomplete or unsupported backups are rejected. Never upload personal backups to GitHub.

Samples and drafts

Sample cleanup removes only unedited examples. Your own or edited items, locations and drafts remain. Batch entry can be saved as a draft and resumed later.

Accounts

Apple and Google sign-in are optional. Signing out preserves inventory on this device. To delete an account, open account management in Me, read the confirmation and reauthenticate if requested. Account deletion and local inventory removal are separate choices; follow the displayed options and back up first. The current test version provides Apple authorization management instructions after deletion; automatic revocation is still being integrated and verified.

Support
