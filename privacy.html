归位隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 31 日

运营者：William。本政策说明归位当前测试版本的数据处理；正式发布前如处理方式变更，会同步更新。公开问题可通过 归位支持 联系我们，请勿在公开问题中提交私人信息。

收纳资料与权限

物品名称、照片、区域、位置、标签、备注和草稿保存在设备上。归位不自动将这些内容上传至应用服务器，也不将照片发送至 AI 模型。拍照时请求相机权限，选择照片或备份时使用系统选择器；拒绝相机权限后仍可手动录入。使用系统键盘听写时，相关处理由系统及其隐私设置控制。

搜索与使用记录用于本机统计，不作为广告追踪数据上传。归位不接入广告 SDK，不出售个人信息，不进行跨应用广告追踪。

可选账号服务

选择 Apple 或 Google 登录时，授权提供方及 Supabase 处理登录所需的账号标识、邮箱、可用的姓名和会话信息，用于认证、安全与账号管理。你可不登录使用本机收纳功能。当前 Supabase 项目位于澳大利亚悉尼，选择登录会涉及境外认证服务；服务商可能为安全运行处理 IP 地址与技术日志。收纳照片和清单不随登录自动同步。

服务商隐私说明：Apple、Google、Supabase。这些服务商按各自政策处理其服务数据。

购买

购买正式开放后由 Apple App Store 处理支付。归位通过 StoreKit 验证产品和购买权益，用于解锁与恢复购买，不收集银行卡信息。具体价格以系统购买确认页面为准。当前购买仍在配置与验证中。

备份与外部支持

备份仅在你主动导出、分享或恢复时处理。完整备份包含收纳资料与照片，请选择可信的保存位置；你选用的文件、云盘或分享服务按其自身政策处理文件。归位无法代替你保管已导出的备份。

访问 GitHub 支持页时，GitHub 按其隐私声明处理访问数据。公开反馈内容对公众可见，请使用脱敏信息。

保留与删除

设备资料保留至你主动删除或系统清除应用数据。退出登录不会删除收纳记录。你可在“我的”中申请删除服务账号，系统可能要求重新验证身份。当前测试版提供 Apple 系统授权管理指引；自动撤销尚未完成上线验证。你自行导出的文件、第三方系统记录与依法必须保留的信息不随应用内账号删除自动消失。服务商安全日志或备份依其保留政策处理。

如需提出访问、更正或删除个人信息的请求，可先通过支持入口联系，并仅提供处理请求所必需的信息。涉及私人资料的请求请勿直接发布在公开 Issue 中。

未成年人和安全

归位面向一般家庭整理场景，不以收集儿童资料为目的。请勿录入不必要的敏感信息。我们使用系统安全存储保存原生登录凭据，网络认证通过 HTTPS 进行；请保护设备锁屏与备份文件。

Guiwei Privacy Policy

Updated: October 31, 2026. Operator: William.

This policy describes the current test version. Material changes will be reflected before release. Contact Guiwei Support without posting private information publicly.

Inventory and permissions

Item names, photos, rooms, locations, tags, notes and drafts are stored on your device. Guiwei does not automatically upload them to its servers or send photos to AI models. Camera permission is requested for taking photos; system pickers handle photo and backup selection. Manual entry works without camera permission. Keyboard dictation is handled by your operating system and its privacy settings.

Search and usage records support local statistics. They are not uploaded for advertising. Guiwei has no advertising SDK and does not sell personal information or track you across apps for advertising.

Optional accounts

If you choose Apple or Google sign-in, the provider and Supabase process account identifiers, email, available name and session information for authentication, security and account management. Local inventory can be used without an account. The current Supabase project is hosted in Sydney, Australia; signing in may transfer authentication data internationally. Providers may process IP addresses and technical logs for security. Signing in does not automatically sync your inventory photos or lists.

Provider policies: Apple, Google, Supabase.

Purchases

When purchasing becomes available, Apple App Store handles payment. Guiwei uses StoreKit to verify product entitlements and restore purchases; it does not collect payment card details. The system purchase sheet shows the applicable price. Purchasing is currently being configured and tested.

Backups and support

Backups are processed when you choose to export, share or restore them. Full backups contain photos and inventory; select a trusted destination. File, cloud and sharing services you choose operate under their own policies. Guiwei cannot retain exported backups on your behalf.

GitHub processes support-site visits under its privacy statement. Issues are public; submit only redacted information.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them or the operating system clears app data. Signing out does not delete inventory. Request account deletion from Me; identity reauthentication may be required. The current test version provides Apple authorization management instructions; automatic revocation is not yet release-verified. Exported files, independent third-party records and information required by law are not automatically removed with your app account. Provider security logs and backups follow their own retention policies.

You may contact support about access, correction or deletion requests. Provide only necessary details and do not post private data in public issues.

Children and security

Guiwei serves general home organization needs and is not designed to collect children's personal information. Avoid unnecessary sensitive details. Native login credentials use system secure storage and authentication uses HTTPS. Protect your device and backup files.
